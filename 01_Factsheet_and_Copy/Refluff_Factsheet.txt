HOW AI WAS USED
As stated on the Steam page: some 3D models in this game, the cleaning tool heads, were
created with an AI 3D model generation tool and then edited by hand. Everything else was
made without generative AI: the simulation and code, the sound effects (real recordings),
the carpet photographs and all store page artwork. No content is generated by AI while you
play.
