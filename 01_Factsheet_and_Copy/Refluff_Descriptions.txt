REFLUFF: CARPET CLEANING ASMR
DESCRIPTIONS, READY TO PASTE
========================================================================

TAGLINE
Filthy in. Fluffy out.

ONE LINE
A first-person ASMR sim about washing filthy rugs clean, on a real simulation of water, foam
and mud.

SHORT (the text on the Steam page)
Refluff is a first-person ASMR sim about running your own rug-washing workshop. Scrape,
soak, foam, scrub and squeegee filthy carpets clean on a real fluid simulation. Get paid for
every percent, upgrade your tools, take on bigger, dirtier rugs.

LONG
Refluff is a calm first-person game about running your own rug-washing workshop. A filthy
rug lands on the tiles, and you walk around it with the tool in your hand and do the job the
way a carpet cleaner does: scrape off the dried crust, vacuum the dust, soak the pile, foam
it, scrub the foam in with a rotary brush, squeegee the slurry onto the floor, pull the
water out and dry the pile until it stands up again.

Nothing here is a picture being wiped away. Water soaks in and wicks through the fibres, dry
dust turns to mud the moment it gets wet, foam heaps up and slowly settles, and whatever you
push off the rug ends up on the floor tiles, where a detergent sprayer and a mop are
waiting.

Every percent you clean pays on the spot. Spend it at the upgrade bench on stronger and
wider tools, or on bigger, dirtier rugs that pay more. There are twelve rugs, from museum
carpets to cartoon designs and a ring with a hole in the middle.

It is made for headphones. Every machine is a recording of a real one: motors spool up and
wind down, the vacuum tube rattles the moment it swallows a crumb, water hisses, foam
fizzes, bristles drag. There is no music, no timer, no enemies and no way to fail.

FEATURES
- First person: walk around the rug with the tool in your hand
- A real-time fluid simulation of water, foam, mud and dried crust
- 10 tools: eight for the carpet, plus a detergent sprayer and a mop for the floor
- 12 rugs: museum carpets, cartoon designs, a round rug and a ring
- Paid for every cleaned percent; POWER and AREA upgrades for every tool; bigger, dirtier rugs to order
- Two inspection lamps that show the dirt and the damp your eyes miss
- Every machine sound is a recording of a real machine, and there is no music
- No enemies, no fail state, no timer running out: clean at your own pace
- A built-in learning guide that teaches every tool
- Single player, mouse and keyboard, interface in 31 languages
